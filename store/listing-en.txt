MedCal – Medical Calculators
---
38 medical calculators for doctors and for everyone. Free, works offline.
---
MedCal brings 38 medical calculators together in one simple app. When you open it, choose who you are: a doctor or other health professional, or a member of the public. You can switch at any time.

FOR DOCTORS (28 TOOLS)
• Kidney: eGFR (CKD-EPI 2021), creatinine clearance (Cockcroft-Gault)
• Heart: mean arterial pressure, QTc, CHA₂DS₂-VA / CHA₂DS₂-VASc, HAS-BLED
• Acute care: Wells DVT / PE with PERC, CURB-65, NEWS2, qSOFA, Glasgow Coma Scale
• Lab: anion gap, corrected calcium, corrected sodium, serum osmolality and osmolal gap
• Body size: BMI (Asian and WHO cut-offs), BSA, ideal and adjusted body weight
• Mother and child: gestational age and due date, Bishop score, maintenance fluids (Holliday-Segar), WHO growth z-scores and MUAC, dehydration and ORS plan
• Burns: Parkland fluids with rule of nines
• Liver and diabetes: APRI and FIB-4, HbA1c to average glucose, Ramadan fasting risk (IDF-DAR 2026)
• Dengue: warning signs and WHO fluid plans
• Infusion and drip rate
• Lab unit converter: conventional (mg/dL) to SI (mmol/L, µmol/L) for common tests

FOR EVERYONE (10 TOOLS)
• BMI and waist check, with Asian cut-offs
• Diabetes risk test
• Blood pressure and blood sugar reading checks
• 10-year heart risk (WHO chart for South Asia)
• Pregnancy due date
• Dengue danger signs
• Ramadan fasting check
• Heat risk check
• Unit converter: sugar, temperature, height and weight

CLEAR RESULTS
Each result shows a coloured band and a short explanation. Every tool shows its formula and its published sources, and you can copy the result. Danger signs show a red "Get medical help now" box with a button to call 1122.

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
No account and no sign-in. The values you enter stay on your phone and are not saved or sent anywhere. The calculators work without internet.

IMPORTANT
MedCal is a reference tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Results do not replace the judgement of a qualified health professional. In an emergency, call 1122 or go to the nearest hospital.

The app is free and shows a small banner ad on the home and settings screens only, never on a calculator.
