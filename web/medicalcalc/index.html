MedCal

Android app by HomiLabs · package com.homilabs.medicalcalc

Health calculators for Pakistan, in two modes. Doctor mode has clinical scores and formulas such as eGFR, CHA2DS2-VA, CURB-65, GCS, APRI/FIB-4, dengue fluids and the Ramadan fasting risk score, with mg/dL units by default. Public mode has simple checks for weight, blood pressure, blood sugar, heart risk, pregnancy, dengue danger signs and heat. Every result shows its published source. The app works offline and has no account.

MedCal is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional. In an emergency in Pakistan call 1122.

Quick answers

  • Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-in.
  • Where do my numbers go? Nowhere. Values you enter stay on your phone and are not sent to us.
  • Who checks the formulas? Each calculator is checked against its published source and reviewed by a doctor before it is switched on in the app.
  • Ads? The app is free and shows Google AdMob banner ads on the home and settings screens only, never next to a result.
  • How do I delete my data? Settings → Clear favourites and recents, or uninstall the app. We hold no data about you.
