Privacy Policy

MedCal · Android · Last updated 9 November 2026

In short: MedCal has no account and no sign-in. The health values you type (such as weight, blood pressure, lab results or symptoms) stay on your phone and are never sent to us or anyone else. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects some device information as explained below. We never sell personal information.

1. Who we are

MedCal is made by HomiLabs Solutions SMC Pvt Ltd ("HomiLabs", "we", "us"). Contact: homilabs.smc@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your phone

WhatWhyLeaves your phone?
Values you enter into a calculator (for example age, weight, blood pressure, lab results, answers to score questions)To work out the result on your phone.No. They are not saved after you leave the calculator.
Your choice of Doctor or Public mode, and your doctor declarationSo the app opens in the right mode.No
Settings (lab units, BMI standard), favourite tools and the last five tools you openedTo make the app quicker to use.No

MedCal does not ask for your name, phone number, email, location, contacts, photos or files, and it has no account. We have no server that receives your health information.

3. Advertising

MedCal is free and shows banner ads through Google AdMob on the home and settings screens only. Ads are never shown on a calculator or next to a result, and ad requests never include the values you enter. Google may collect the device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from the IP address, and how you interact with ads, and may use these to personalise ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the United Kingdom and Switzerland) you are asked for consent first and can change your choice later in Settings → Privacy and ad choices. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads).

4. Permissions

  • Internet and network state – only for ads. The calculators work offline.
  • Advertising ID – used by Google AdMob.

5. Who else handles data

Google AdMob (ads) is the only service provider that receives data from the app. We do not sell personal information and do not share it with anyone else, except where the law requires.

6. How long we keep it

We keep nothing. Settings, favourites and recents stay on your phone until you clear them in Settings → Clear favourites and recents, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Change your ad consent in Settings → Privacy and ad choices (where shown).
  • Clear favourites and recents in Settings, or uninstall the app to remove everything.
  • If you have a question about your data, write to homilabs.smc@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days.

8. Children

MedCal is intended for adults (18 and over). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

9. Changes

We update this policy before a version that changes what the app uses is released. The date above shows the latest change.
